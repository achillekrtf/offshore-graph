Achille, about 1 minute. Step 1: the file is a graph stored in CSV. Every row has a sourceID, the leak it comes from, so one line of pandas keeps the Panama Papers. We keep only the three link types that attach an entity to the network, officer_of, intermediary_of and registered_address, and only links whose two ends are in our subset. That gives 213,634 entities, 238,402 officers, 14,110 intermediaries and 93,454 addresses. Step 2: officers, intermediaries and addresses are connectors. We put them in one table of connector and entity pairs, remove duplicates, and add each pair as an edge. The graph is bipartite: entities on one side, connectors on the other, never an edge inside a side. It has 468,623 nodes and 511,687 edges, undirected and simple.
