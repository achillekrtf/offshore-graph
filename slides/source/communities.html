Adrian, about 1 minute. To adapt to your own words. We run Louvain on the weighted entity projection, with resolution 1.0. The 129,607 isolated entities cannot belong to any community, so they are excluded, which leaves 84,027 entities. The main result is on the giant component, because a component of two entities sharing one officer is trivially a pure community and would inflate every score. Five seeds give 158 to 160 communities, a modularity of 0.957 and a pairwise NMI of 0.98, so the partition is stable. python-louvain gives the same modularity and an NMI of 0.99 with the NetworkX partition.
