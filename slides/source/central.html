Jad, about 1 minute 30. To adapt to your own words. Three measures on the bipartite graph. Degree is the number of entities an intermediary or an officer is linked to. PageRank gives more weight to a node whose neighbors are themselves important, with a damping of 0.85. Betweenness is the share of shortest paths that go through a node; an exact run needs a shortest-path tree from each of the 311,000 nodes, so we estimate it from 50 random sources, with a fixed seed. Results: Orion House Services in Hong Kong is linked to 7,016 entities, Mossack Fonseca Panama to 4,364. The top 15 intermediaries by degree and by PageRank are the same 15 names, rank correlation 0.92. By betweenness, the Singapore and UK offices of Mossack Fonseca come first and Orion House third. Among officers, Mossfon Subscribers in Samoa is linked to 3,882 entities against 368 for the next one, a nominee company on a large share of shortest paths. On the projection the rankings differ: none of the top 20 entities of the bipartite graph appears in the top 20 of the projection.
