Jad, about 55 seconds. To adapt to your own words. Two measures on the bipartite graph. Degree is the number of entities an intermediary or an officer is linked to. PageRank gives more weight to a node whose neighbors are themselves important, with a damping of 0.85. Results: Orion House Services in Hong Kong is linked to 7,016 entities, Mossack Fonseca Panama to 4,364, then Prime Corporate Solutions and Offshore Business Consultant. The top 15 intermediaries by degree and by PageRank are the same 15 names, with a rank correlation of 0.92 over all intermediaries.
