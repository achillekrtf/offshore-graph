What we found, and its limits

Findings

  • A few intermediaries and one nominee officer carry the network
  • Communities follow the intermediaries, then officer countries, far above jurisdictions
  • The network is fragmented: 61 % of entities are isolated in the projection

Limits

  • One leak, the clients of one law firm, data cut-off in 2015
  • Hub cap is a modeling choice that shapes distances and isolation
  • Betweenness is sampled, reliable at the top of the ranking
  • Presence in the leak does not imply wrongdoing
