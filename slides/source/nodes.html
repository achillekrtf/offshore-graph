Achille · 3 / 11 · Panama Papers subset of the ICIJ bulk file

Achille, about 55 seconds. The ICIJ file is a graph stored in CSV, with four types of nodes. An entity is the offshore company itself: for instance a company registered in Samoa. An officer is a person or a company that owns, directs or fronts an entity; some officers are nominees, like Mossfon Subscribers in Samoa, linked to 3,882 entities. An intermediary is a law firm, bank or agent that ordered the structure for its clients; the biggest, Orion House Services in Hong Kong, is linked to 7,016 entities. An address is a postal address, and most address links start from an officer, 145,795 out of 151,105. Three link types connect an entity to the rest: officer_of, intermediary_of and registered_address. We call officers, intermediaries and addresses the connectors, because they are what links two entities.
