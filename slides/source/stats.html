Distances are computed per component, in hops. On the projection giant component the computation is exact: diameter 30, radius 16. On the bipartite giant component, with 311,000 nodes, an exact all-pairs computation is too heavy, so we give bounds: a diameter of at least 37 and a radius of at most 20.
