Achille, about 50 seconds. Our question: who structures the offshore network of the Panama Papers? We look for the central intermediaries, officers and jurisdictions, then check whether Louvain communities follow the jurisdictions, the countries of the officers or the intermediaries. The data is the ICIJ Offshore Leaks bulk file, Panama Papers subset, under ODbL and CC BY-SA. It is a graph stored in CSV with four node types. An entity is the offshore company itself. An officer owns, directs or fronts an entity; some are nominees, like Mossfon Subscribers in Samoa, linked to 3,882 entities. An intermediary is a law firm, bank or agent that ordered the structure for its clients; the biggest, Orion House Services in Hong Kong, is linked to 7,016 entities. An address is a postal address; most address links start from an officer. Three link types attach an entity to the network: officer_of, intermediary_of and registered_address. We call officers, intermediaries and addresses the connectors, because they are what links two entities. Panama Papers only: the Pandora entities have no relationships in the bulk file.
