The file is a graph in CSV: entities are the offshore companies, officers are owners, directors or nominees, intermediaries are the firms that ordered the structure, and addresses. We build two graphs. The bipartite graph links each entity to its connectors. The projection links two entities when they share a connector, with the number of shared connectors as the weight. In the small example on the left, E1 and E2 share Marie, E2 and E3 share Cabinet X, and E4 has no partner, so it is isolated. One modeling choice matters: a connector linked to d entities creates d(d-1)/2 edges, so an intermediary with 7,016 entities alone would add about 24 million edges. We cap the hubs: officers above 500 entities, intermediaries and addresses above 50 are ignored when linking entities.
